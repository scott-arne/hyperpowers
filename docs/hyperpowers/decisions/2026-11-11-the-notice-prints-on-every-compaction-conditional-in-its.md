# The notice prints on every compaction, conditional in its text on following a skill

- Status: accepted
- Spec: docs/hyperpowers/specs/2026-11-11-compaction-reread-notice-design.md (decision 3)
- Recorded: 2026-11-14; notes from the branch diff by a subagent at finish; plan docs/hyperpowers/plans/2026-11-11-compaction-reread-notice.md removed at finish

## Decision

**The notice prints on every compaction, conditional in its text on
following a skill.** It does not depend on an SDD ledger or a git
repository. Brainstorming and finishing sessions are cut short too, and
stale text affects every hyperpowers skill a long session loaded
(human partner, 2026-11-11). Rejected: appending it to the ledger notice
only, which covers SDD and leaves stale brainstorming and finishing
copies without help.
Reversed by decision 11 (2026-11-13); the notice did not ship.

## What implementation changed

The notice did not ship, so no compaction prints it. Decision 11 reversed this.
